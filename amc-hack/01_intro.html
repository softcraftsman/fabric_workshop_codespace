Welcome: Build Confidence, Then Build Freely

This workshop has two parts. First you complete four short, guided labs that build on one another. Then you split into teams for the open hack and apply what you learned to a healthcare-quality challenge of your choice.

What you will do

The guided labs

  1. Explore the data with Copilot in a notebook — learn what is in the Gold tables by asking questions in plain language.
  2. Make a semantic model AI-ready — add the descriptions, synonyms, and instructions that make AI answers trustworthy.
  3. Build a report with Copilot — turn the model into a visual page.
  4. Build a Data Agent — let people ask questions of your model in conversation.

Every lab follows the same format: a goal, what you need, numbered steps, an expected result, and a "If you get stuck" checkpoint. You are not expected to memorize anything. The labs use the Readmissions slice of the data so that everyone gets comparable results.

The tools you will use

  • Microsoft Fabric — lakehouses, notebooks, semantic models, reports, and Data Agents.
  • Copilot in Fabric — AI assistance inside notebooks, reports, and models.
  • GitHub Copilot CLI — AI assistance from the terminal in your Codespace.

How the pages unlock

Pages are released one at a time as the workshop progresses. Your facilitator gives you a code to open the next page. If a page is locked, you are waiting for the group, not missing something.

Ground rules

  • Patient-level data in this workshop is synthetic. CMS facility-level results are real public data. Page 06 explains how to keep them separate.
  • Ask for help early. A stuck step is a facilitator's job, not a personal failure.
  • Every lab can be recovered from its checkpoint. Do not skip ahead because you fell behind.
